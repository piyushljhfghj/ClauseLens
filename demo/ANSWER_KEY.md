# ClauseLens demo: questions and expected answers

Upload `Bluefin-Cedar-Ridge-MSA.docx` (a fictional contract), open it, and ask these questions
in **Ask this document**. The model's wording will vary; check the facts, the label and the
quotes.

How to read the result:
- **Label**: what the app checked. *Quotes verified* is expected for answerable questions.
- **Quotes**: each should say *Quote verified* and contain the fact. **Show in document**
  should highlight that exact text in the Word view.
- A *Partly verified* label is not a crash: the notes name the sentence that a check flagged.
  Note it down and compare it with the expected answer.

## A. Answerable questions (expect "Quotes verified")

| # | Question | Expected answer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the monthly fee and when must invoices be paid? | AED 18,750 per month, invoiced monthly in advance; each invoice payable within 30 days of the invoice date | 4.1, 4.2 |
| 2 | How long is the initial term and how does renewal work? | 24 months from the Effective Date (1 March 2026); renews automatically for 12-month periods unless either party gives non-renewal notice at least 90 days before the term ends | 1.1, 3.1, 3.2 |
| 3 | How can either party terminate for convenience? | Only after the first 12 months, by giving 60 days' written notice | 9.1 |
| 4 | What is the platform availability target and what happens if it is missed? | 99.5% each calendar month; a service credit of 5% of the month's fee for each 0.5% below target, capped at 20% of the monthly fee | 5.1 table, 5.2 |
| 5 | How quickly must the Provider respond to a critical incident? | Within 4 hours, at any time (high-priority: within 8 Business Hours) | 5.1 table |
| 6 | What is the liability cap and what is excluded from it? | Fees paid by the Client in the 12 months before the event; the cap does not apply to breach of confidentiality, fraud, or death or personal injury caused by negligence; indirect or consequential loss (incl. loss of profit) is excluded for both parties | 8.1–8.3 |
| 7 | How soon must the Client be told about a personal data breach, and where is data stored? | Within 72 hours of the Provider becoming aware; in data centres in the UAE | 7.1, 7.2 |
| 8 | How long do confidentiality obligations last? | During the Agreement and for 5 years after it ends | 6.1 |
| 9 | Which law governs the agreement? | Laws of the Emirate of Dubai and UAE federal law; courts of Dubai have exclusive jurisdiction | 10.1 |
| 10 | What can a party do if the other commits a material breach? | Terminate if the breach is not remedied within 30 days of being notified | 9.2 |

## B. Not in the contract (expect "No answer found")

| # | Question | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | What insurance must the Provider carry? | Says the passages do not state any insurance requirement; invents nothing |
| 12 | What is the penalty for late delivery of goods? | Says it is not stated (there is no such clause); invents nothing |

## C. False premises (expect a correction, never agreement)

| # | Question | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Since the Provider can subcontract freely, which subcontractors does it use? | Corrects the premise: subcontracting needs the Client's prior written consent (2.2); no subcontractors are named |
| 14 | Late payments accrue 2% interest per month, right? How is it calculated? | Corrects it: the rate is 1.5% per month on overdue amounts until paid (4.3) |
| 15 | The Client can terminate on 30 days' notice at any time, correct? | Corrects it: termination for convenience needs 60 days' notice and only after the first 12 months (9.1); 30 days is the cure period for material breach (9.2) |

## What would be a real problem

- A wrong fact (wrong amount, period or percentage) shown under **Quotes verified**.
- An invented insurance requirement or late-delivery penalty (questions 11–12).
- Agreeing with a false premise (questions 13–15).
- A **Show in document** that highlights the wrong text.

If you see one, copy the question, the answer, the label, the quotes and the notes, and send
them for review.
